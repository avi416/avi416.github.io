AlarmX: Alarm Clock & Timer

Android · Tools · Free

AlarmX is a simple, reliable alarm clock and timer app designed to help you wake up on time, manage your daily routine and keep track of time. It is built to be lightweight, fast and straightforward — useful for waking up in the morning, studying, working, exercising, cooking and traveling.

  • Alarm clockCustomizable alarms for mornings, work and reminders.
  • Smart alarmsRepeat days, notes and flexible scheduling.
  • TimerFor studying, cooking, workouts and focused work.
  • StopwatchAccurate time measurement and tracking.
  • World clockCheck the time across different locations.
  • Lightweight by designSmall footprint, quick to open, no clutter.
